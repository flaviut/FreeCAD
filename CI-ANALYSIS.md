**Protect the compiler caches first. Then shorten Ubuntu’s warm path and profile native Windows.** A warm Ubuntu compile took **11:27** at the median; a zero-hit compile took **1:02:13**. Native Windows finished last in **12 of 18** full approved runs, by a median **17:36** when it did. Those are different problems: cache availability drives the long tail; preprocessing, linking, setup, and tests drive the everyday wait.
